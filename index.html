FAQ

What is CryptoScreener PRO?

A real-time cryptocurrency screener and multi-chart terminal. It displays live candlestick charts on multiple timeframes and ranks Binance pairs by 24h change, NATR volatility and trading volume.

Where does the data come from?

Market data is streamed in real time from Binance public market-data endpoints (REST + WebSocket). No account or API key is required for the free tier.

What is NATR?

NATR (Normalized Average True Range) measures volatility as a percentage of price, letting you compare volatility across different coins.

How much does PRO cost?

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
